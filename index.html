Privacy Policy

1. Overview

This website is a personal portfolio. It is accompanied by a Telegram bot that lets people leave reviews and quick ratings. This policy explains what data is involved and how it is handled.

2. This website

  • The website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or user accounts.
  • It has no forms and does not send any data from your browser to me. It only loads the published reviews and ratings files.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to its own privacy policy.

3. Telegram bot: reviews

When you send /feedback, the bot stores and publishes:

  • your Telegram username (or your first name if you have no username);
  • the text of your review;
  • the date it was submitted.

Reviews are public: they are shown on this website and stored in a public GitHub repository. Do not include personal or sensitive information in a review.

4. Telegram bot: quick ratings

When you use /fastf, your rating (1–10) is stored together with a one-way hash of your Telegram user ID. Your name, username and ID are not stored with it, and the website shows only the average score. The hash is used only so that each person has a single rating that can be changed or removed.

5. Other data

To prevent spam, the bot temporarily keeps your Telegram user ID in memory for a short cooldown between reviews. It is not written to any file and is lost when the bot restarts.

6. Retention and removal

Reviews and ratings are kept until they are removed. See the "Data Removal" section below for how to delete them.

7. Third parties

Your use of the bot is also subject to the policies of Telegram and GitHub. I do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

8. Children

The bot and website are not intended for children.

9. Changes

I may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
